Neat glass-style frames for your apps, folders and notes on your Windows desktop.
Works on Windows 10 and 11.

## Features

### Smooth and stable
- Dragging frames is smooth, with no jitter.
- Locked frames stay where you put them, even after Win+D, sleep, or screen changes.

### Shortcuts that behave
- Drag apps in from the desktop and they move into the frame, like cut and paste.
- Drag them out again to take them back.
- Frame types: shortcut frames, folder frames that mirror a real folder, and note frames.

### Organize your way
- Roll frames up and down, automatically when you point at them or manually.
- Rename a frame by double-clicking its title.
- Customize colors, transparency, rounded corners and icon size.
- Icons match your desktop icon size by default.

### Privacy
- Hide a frame behind a password, with a hint and a recovery word.
- Frame contents are encrypted.

### Smart auto-sort
- Choose Auto, Manual or Remain as is.
- Sort new files into folders by your own rules, with undo.

### Animations you choose
- Separate styles and speeds for frame roll, menus and app icons.
- Includes a heartbeat pump for icons.

### Also
- Tray icon with quick controls and hotkeys.
- Can start with Windows.
- Tells you when a new version is available.
